Research profile

Dr. Ruocco’s research concerns the analysis, design and implementation of time-sensitive and time-aware computer systems. His PhD introduced temporal reflection, a class of computational reflection through which a system can represent, observe and control its own temporal behaviour; he designed and implemented a reflective real-time scheduler for the L4 microkernel. At NICTA he analysed and improved the real-time properties of general-purpose L4 microkernels. At A*STAR and NTU he worked on systems software and programming toolchains for non-volatile memory, storage systems, RISC-V platforms and AI accelerators, including the port of MbedOS and Linux device drivers to the ECS-DoT SoC and its AI accelerator. He is currently a Senior Software Engineer at EMASS, building the full software stack (from the development boards’ boot-agent firmware to the SDK), the CI/CD build and test pipeline, and the IT/DevOps infrastructure for the ECS-DoT ultra-low-power AI SoC used by the EMASS engineering team and its customers; he planned its Zephyr RTOS port and delivered it with a newly formed team he mentored. His work has appeared at IEEE RTSS, ISCAS, TCAS-I and JETCAS and in the EURASIP Journal on Embedded Systems, and has led to a US patent and several technology disclosures licensed to EMASS Pte Ltd.
